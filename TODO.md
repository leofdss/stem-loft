## Where the board lives

The v1 task board moved to [OpenSpec](openspec/README.md): every task is a
change under `openspec/changes/task-NNN-<slug>/` — its proposal, its
acceptance criteria (`tasks.md`) and the behavior it adds — with status,
branch, dependencies and playbooks in
[`openspec/board.toml`](openspec/board.toml). Finished tasks are archived
under `openspec/changes/archive/`, and the behavior they added becomes part
of `openspec/specs/`.

- Visual board: [`docs/kanban.html`](docs/kanban.html), generated by
  [`scripts/openspec_to_kanban.py`](scripts/openspec_to_kanban.py).
- From a terminal: `scripts/openspec.sh list`, then
  `scripts/openspec.sh show <change>`.
- How to propose, start, finish and archive a task (in both languages):
  [`openspec/README.md`](openspec/README.md#lifecycle).

## Icebox (post-v1, not yet broken into tasks)

Out of scope for the board above, but already acknowledged in
[Current status and future work](docs/architecture.md#current-status-and-future-work) —
don't start these without first turning them into their own OpenSpec
change (`task-NNN-<slug>`) with acceptance criteria:

- Automatic stem separation (`Separation Client` in the core, `Automatic
  Separation` in the UI, the external `Stem Separation API`) — needs an
  async job pattern (polling or webhook) not yet designed.
- Editing chords/tablature in `Chord/Tab View`, with the core writing the
  `.cho` back (reusing the atomic-write pattern from TASK-002).
